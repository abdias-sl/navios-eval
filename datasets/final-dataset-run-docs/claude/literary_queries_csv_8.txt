Traditionally, eggs were broken at the larger end. However, when the grandfather of the current Lilliputian emperor cut his finger while breaking an egg in this manner, his father issued an edict requiring all subjects to break eggs at the smaller end, under severe penalties. This decree caused significant public resentment, leading to six rebellions in which one emperor lost his life and another his crown. These civil conflicts were continuously encouraged by the rulers of Blefuscu, who offered refuge to exiles from Lilliput. According to estimates, eleven thousand people chose death rather than submit to breaking their eggs at the smaller end."
